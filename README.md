# Multimodal Drone Controller (working title)

This is a research science repo exploring voice and brain signals as a bridge between human operators and tactical drone swarms in high-risk, zero-connectivity environments. 

We envision a future where a goal can be as simple as a quiet thought, and the swarm figures out the rest. From a spoken high-level strategy or a couple glances on a monitor and a thought, the ambition is to make these intent actionable in the physical world, in the most critical situations where lives are at stake.

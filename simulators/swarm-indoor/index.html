What you are looking at

A floor seen from above. Rooms, 0.2 m walls, doors under a 2.0 m lintel, tables (0.8 m) and shelves (2.2 m). The coloured cones show what each drone camera can see at its height: walls, shelves and door lintels block it.

Every 0.5 s the master (M) picks a tactic template for the current order and sends each follower a vector over a lossy radio. Yellow dashed lines are those vectors; squares are the slots. Amber rooms are where the person could be, green rooms were cleared since he was last seen.

Safety rules run on board: never closer than 1.2 m to a person; 1.5 m in pressure tactics (drive out, keep out at the door) and 2.5 m otherwise (the dashed ring); 1 m between drones, 0.4 m from walls, one drone per doorway, slow in doors. The person model and the tactics are explained in docs/TACTICS.md; the training records in docs/DATASET_PLAN.md.

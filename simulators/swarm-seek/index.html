What is different from Swarm Guard

Over many runs with the 8 m alarm the drones turn away about 3 intruders in 4, with a vague alarm about 2 in 3, and in silent patrol about 1 in 3: 3 cameras cannot watch 100 m of fence at once. Every number comes from the laws below, so change a slider and watch the outcome move.

Take the keys: press Control intruder (or c), then click or tap any spot on the ground, a staircase or the dock: he runs there on the shortest walkable route, stairs and doors included. A yellow ring marks the goal and a yellow line the route. Dragging still orbits the camera. The arrow keys also walk him (relative to the camera, hold space for stairs). You run at 80 % of the drones\' top speed, set by the slider in law L7. The p key pauses. Inside the building no camera from outside can see you, so the drones must come in through a door, at 2.2 m, and search room by room. Follow keeps the camera on you.

Nobody sends the drones a position. The fence sensor says only "something crossed this segment". The drones lift off their pads, climb to a height chosen from how uncertain they are, and scan with their own cameras. Each drone keeps a probability map of where the person could be, moves the mass at walking speed, and lowers it wherever a camera saw nothing.

The first camera to detect the person broadcasts the sighting. The others drop their search, one tracks from 4 m above, two take flank positions 3 m ahead of the person. Two drones within 4.5 m for 6 s and the person gives up. If the person hides under a tree, the track is lost, the belief re-spreads from the last sighting and the search starts again, locally.

How it works

The loop. You speak a goal (buttons, keys 1 to 4; ← → rotate the formation by 15°). The intruder has its own mind: picks the closest door, hides under trees when a drone comes near, backs off when pressed, gives up after 3 pushes or 6 s of challenge, goes inside on a breach and comes out again.

The split. Every 0.5 s the master picks a template for the goal, computes 3 points, assigns them and issues 3 full 3-D vectors. It keeps 1 and radios 2 to the slaves, with latency and packet loss (law L4m).

The rotor layer. A slave never plans. It sets target = own position + received vector and runs a quadrotor controller: thrust, roll and pitch with an attitude time constant, then a 4-rotor mixer. The arrows in the scene are those vectors, with 1 m ticks. The master runs the same loop on its own vector.

Test bench. Keys stand in for your 2 EEG classes, Sim BCI is a classifier with accuracy and latency, Replay takes a timestamped log, Live takes a websocket. Sweep finds the surprise budget that maximises time in view.
